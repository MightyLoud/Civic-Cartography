# Gillespie County, Texas

Gillespie County is modeled as an eleven-office county release with one countywide geography and four Commissioner-precinct geographies.

## Active elected County Surveyor

The County Surveyor is an active elected office, not a historical placeholder. Gillespie County identifies Don Kuhlmann, RPLS as County Surveyor and lists the office among elected officials. Texas Constitution Article XVI § 44 and Natural Resources Code § 23.011 preserve the election, four-year term, and professional-surveyor qualification.

The County Engineering Department is separate. Melissa Eckert, PE, CFM is the County Engineer, but that administrative and technical role neither replaces nor duplicates the elected County Surveyor. Kuhlmann's 2026 campaign filing is retained only as future-election evidence and does not create another current office, holder, or term.

## Current bounded scope

- County Judge
- Commissioner Precincts 1–4
- Sheriff
- County Clerk
- District Clerk
- Tax Assessor-Collector
- County Treasurer
- County Surveyor

County Attorney, constables, justices of the peace, judicial offices, and County Engineer remain explicit non-scope evidence.

## Geography

The countywide feature is the current Census TIGERweb polygon for GEOID `48171`. Commissioner geometry is an authoritative composite. The pinned official Gillespie County Commissioner PDF controls district assignment, while the Texas Legislative Council's current 2026 primary voting-precinct shapefile controls exact polygon geometry. All 13 current voting precincts are assigned to one of four Commissioner precincts; the dissolved output has zero gaps, zero overlaps, and zero union difference.
